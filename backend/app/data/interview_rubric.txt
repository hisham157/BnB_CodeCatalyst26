Assess only professional, role-relevant evidence in the answer.
Relevance: Does the answer address the question and role context?
Specificity: Are actions, responsibilities and constraints concrete?
Reasoning: Does the candidate explain choices and trade-offs?
Role depth: Is the explanation sound for this profession and difficulty?
Evidence: Are examples and outcomes explained without inventing verification?
Clarity: Is the meaning understandable? Do not penalize accent or personal style.
Ask clarification when evidence is missing; do not treat missing evidence as proof of inability.
Scores from 0 to 5 are provisional interview signals, not validated employability measurements.
Never make hiring/rejection decisions or infer protected traits.
